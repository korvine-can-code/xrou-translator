DVANDVA XROU-HAXA TRANSLATOR — VERSION 22
Casual/Formal Xrou Register and Canonical Vocabulary

What's new
- Casual/Formal speech toggle on the Translator tab.
- Casual I = adam and we = adamys, regardless of gender.
- Formal I = adam (masculine), adar (feminine), adal (neutral); we = Xcsadam.
- The formal gender selector defaults to neutral (adal) until deliberately selected.
- Both speech registers are recognized in Xrou → English translation.
- Speech register applies to translated text and phrasebook / sentence-pattern examples.
- Dictionary terms added or corrected: mitr, vai-mitr, bizaza, Xatra, Xatrani, vaz, kori.
- Earlier meanings retained where distinct: draugov = the act of lying, natha = master.
- Full dictionary, phrasebook, sentence patterns, community translation request, favorites,
  history, synonyms and art remain installed.

How to install
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. In your GitHub Pages repository, upload/replace all extracted files.
3. Keep your existing CNAME file to maintain xrou-haxa.com.
4. Commit changes; if old version is cached, hard refresh or open a private window.

Note
Casual/formal changes apply only to first-person personal pronouns; they do NOT
change adam- possessive compounds or unrelated proper names. The dictionary
spelling adas was superseded by the user-canonical adal.

The possessive "our" also follows the register: da-adamys (casual) / da-Xcsadam (formal).
